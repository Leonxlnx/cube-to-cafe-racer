# Audio credits: aluminium cube to cafe racer (45 s product film)

Every file was downloaded on 2026-11-17 from a free-audio source whose licence allows use in a video posted online. Nothing was synthesized locally; the only processing was ffmpeg/numpy trimming of leading silence and trailing silence, short fades at the cut points, resampling to 48 kHz/24-bit WAV, and a gain change on some files (down so that no file peaks above -1 dBFS, or up for a few very quiet originals; see `notes` in manifest.json). Licences were read on each source page (Freesound licences re-checked one by one on 2026-11-17).

Licences used: **CC0 1.0** (no attribution), **CC BY 4.0** / **CC BY 3.0** (attribution required), **Mixkit Sound Effects Free License** and **Mixkit Stock Music Free License** (commercial use, including online video, allowed; no attribution required; the files may not be redistributed or resold on their own, see https://mixkit.co/license/ and https://mixkit.co/llm-info/). There is no NC, ND or SA material.

Freesound files are the site's public HQ previews (mp3, about 128-190 kbps, original sample rate); the original WAV uploads need a login, which was not used. Mixkit SFX are the WAV downloads except four that only served a preview mp3 (noted per file).

**How the sounds were judged:** the sourcing agent cannot listen. Choices rest on the title, the uploader's description and tags, and measurements (envelope, onsets, noise floor, clipping, spectrum, loudness). Audition before you lock the cut.
